## Overview

Team health indicators are tracked by the Team Lead to maintain a sustainable and productive work environment. These metrics are **non-penalized** — they exist to inform leadership decisions, not to score individuals.

---

## 📊 Tracked Indicators

| Indicator | Description |
| --------- | ----------- |
| **Workload Balance** | Ensuring tasks are evenly distributed across the team |
| **Burnout Signals** | Monitoring overtime, stress levels, and sustained high workload |
| **PR Overload** | Detecting when individuals have too many PRs pending review |

---

## Purpose

- Early detection of team friction or fatigue
- Data-driven sprint planning adjustments
- Supporting a healthy, sustainable engineering culture

---

## 🎯 Final Goal

Build a high-performing team that delivers:

- Scalable systems
- Clean architecture
- High-quality code
- Production-ready features

---

> 🚀 **Golden Rule:** *"It's not about finishing tasks… it's about finishing them **correctly, cleanly, and sustainably**."*
